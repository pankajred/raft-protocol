Q&A

Scenario-based Raft questions for deeper understanding.

Use the category filters to focus on elections, deep technical behavior, YugabyteDB, operations, or management explanations.

What exactly triggers a new election?

A follower starts an election when it does not receive valid heartbeats or AppendEntries before its randomized election timeout expires.

Why can a stale leader not safely keep writing?

It cannot commit without majority. When it sees a higher term, it must step down.

Why is replicated not the same as committed?

Replication means copies exist. Commitment means the quorum rule makes the entry durable across future leaders.

What is the Figure-8 safety lesson?

Older-term entries can be widely replicated but still not safely committed without the current-term quorum rule.

Why does tablet leader distribution matter?

Leadership placement controls where write coordination lands. Skewed leaders create hotspots.

How do you troubleshoot leader churn?

Check packet loss, CPU stalls, disk latency, heartbeat timing, overloaded RPC workers, and quorum health.

How do you explain Raft to leadership?

Raft gives the system one safe authority per replicated group and waits for a majority before confirming critical changes.
