Basics

Raft fundamentals, formulas, and safety rules.

Start here for roles, terms, majority math, log matching, and the practical reason Raft is easier to explain than Paxos.

Core Model

Raft turns consensus into understandable parts.

Leader

The leader receives writes, appends log entries, and asks followers to replicate them.

Followers

Followers accept valid leader messages and start elections only when heartbeats disappear.

Terms

Terms are logical epochs. Seeing a higher term forces stale leaders and candidates to step down.

Formulas

The math behind quorum and failure tolerance.

Quorum

quorum = floor(N / 2) + 1

A majority must agree before the cluster can safely elect or commit.

Failures

f = floor((N - 1) / 2)

A 5-node cluster can tolerate 2 failed voting members and still keep quorum.

Sizing

N = 2f + 1

To tolerate f failures, use at least 2f + 1 voting members.

Log Matching Rules

  • Followers reject AppendEntries if the previous index and term do not match.
  • Uncommitted divergent suffixes can be overwritten by the leader.
  • Committed entries must appear in future leaders.

Raft vs Paxos

Raft is designed to be equivalent to Paxos in fault tolerance and performance goals, but decomposes consensus into leader election, log replication, and safety so teams can understand and implement it more easily.
