Replication And Commit Flow

Replication creates copies. Commitment requires majority acknowledgment and is the safety boundary.

Leader path
1. Client sends write2. Leader appends entry3. Leader sends AppendEntries
Follower path
4. Followers check prevLogIndex / prevLogTerm5. Matching followers append and ACK6. Majority ACK means commit
Important: replicated is not the same as committed. The leader can respond safely only after quorum is reached.

Partition Scenarios

What actually happens when the network gets weird.

Progress depends on a majority being available. Safety does not depend on timing, but availability does.

Leader isolated in minority partition

The old leader cannot commit. The majority side can elect a new leader. Minority-only entries remain uncommitted and may be rolled back.

Follower isolated

The cluster remains available if the remaining nodes still form quorum. The isolated follower catches up later.

Delayed heartbeats

Packet loss or stalls can cause unnecessary elections and latency spikes even without a hard partition.

Asymmetric partition

Raft converges because terms and quorum depend on successful round trips, not one-way reachability.

No side has quorum

No leader can safely commit. Progress stops until quorum returns.
