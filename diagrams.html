Failure Handling

The majority side can keep working; the minority side cannot commit safely.

Minority

Old leader may be alive but cannot gather quorum.

No safe commits

Majority

A new leader can be elected in a higher term.

Writes can continue

Recovery

Old leader steps down and uncommitted divergent entries are repaired.
